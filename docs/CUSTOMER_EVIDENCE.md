## What the evidence supports

The correspondence supports four conclusions:

1. Michigan's PFAS tier classification and state-reporting workflow already operate without
   the proposed product at the programs represented here.
2. Some wastewater facilities do not land apply, so they have no need for field-readiness
   software.
3. A stronger problem lead exists around connecting plants and farmers and assembling field
   information for land application.
4. The team has not validated repeated labor, purchasing authority, budget, pricing, or
   willingness to run a paid pilot.

The current buyer hypothesis should focus on third-party land-application contractors and
utilities that actively manage land application. Future interviews should test how those
organizations source fields, maintain agreements and soil tests, calculate usable acreage,
select fields for each application event, and document human approval.
